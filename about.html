About the Institute

The Institute of Building Design (IBD) is an official online educational collaboration between An Architect and the South African Institute of Building Design (SAIBD), a recognised voluntary association established under the Architectural Profession Act No. 44 of 2000.

Act 44 of 2000 Recognition SAIBD is a formal voluntary association recognised under the Architectural Profession Act.
SACAP CPD Aligned Fully compliant with the 10 Core Professional Competencies established by SACAP.
Peer Review Board Independent assessment board comprising registered architects, engineers, and technologists.
National Industry Network Direct connections to leading commercial firms, municipal bodies, and private practices.
Origins & Purpose

The Institute's Story

As artificial intelligence, algorithmic design, and automated computational tools rapidly enter architectural and engineering practice, professionals face a significant challenge: generic commercial courses provide surface-level tricks with zero statutory accreditation, leaving practitioners stranded in a CPD compliance nightmare.

Founded by Videsh Boodu (President of SAIBD) and Akheel Khan (Founder of An Architect), the Institute of Building Design was established to create an institutional home where foundational architectural theory, emerging technology, and professional practice converge.

Through substantive programmes vetted by practising leaders, IBD ensures that South African professionals do not waste money on arbitrary tutorials, but instead master accredited competencies that advance architectural practice and safeguard professional standing.

Our Vision

Redefinition of professional education in South Africa through first principles thinking, applied innovation, and accessibility, with the aim of creating technological advancements and economic prosperity within the built environment.

Our Mission

To build a local and international ecosystem of professionals capable of critical reasoning, technologically empowered design, and responsibly informed leadership within the built environment.
