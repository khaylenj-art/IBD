Comprehensive 3-Part Curriculum

Most AI courses offer superficial prompt tricks. IBD teaches you the mathematical foundations, perceptual models, and architectural logic needed to command technology as a thinking partner.

01
Weeks 1–2 • Sessions 1–2

Part 1: Thinking

History, definitions, and foundational models of AI. Patrick Winston’s Triadic Model of Human Intelligence. Deconstruction of Large Language Models (LLMs): tokenisation, latent vectors, attention mechanisms, and temperature. Applying First Principles Thinking to architectural problem definitions.

02
Weeks 2–3 • Sessions 3–4

Part 2: Perception

Machine perception and computational statistics: Linear Regression, K-Nearest Neighbours (KNN), and Random Forests. Transition into Deep Learning: artificial neural networks from Perceptrons to convolutional networks (LeNet and AlexNet). Practical programming and computer vision for spatial layouts.

03
Weeks 4–5 • Sessions 5–8

Part 3: Action

Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning in real-world practice. Training datasets vs testing datasets. Distinguishing tools from systemic technologies. Deploying custom Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) workflows using diffusion and multimodal models. Peer review project defense.
