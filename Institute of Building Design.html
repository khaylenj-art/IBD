Why choose the Institute

About the Institute

The Institute of Building Design (IBD) is an official online division of the South African Institute of Building Design (SAIBD), focused on alternative, substantive and relevant professional level programmes aligned to CPD requirements.

Founded by Videsh Boodu (President of SAIBD) and Akheel Khan (Founder of An Architect), and driven by the SAIBD team, the IBD offers a platform where technology, theory and practice converge.

Educational philosophy

IBD promotes a pedagogical model where technology becomes a thinking partner, not a replacement. Each course integrates First Principles Thinking, Technological Application and Peer Review as a method of soundness for competence.

Vision

Redefinition of professional education in South Africa through first principles thinking, applied innovation and accessibility, with the aim of creating technological advancements and economic prosperity within the built environment.

Mission

To build a local and international ecosystem of professionals capable of critical reasoning, technologically empowered design and responsibly informed leadership within the built environment.

Strategic partners

  • SAIBD: accreditation and governance
  • An Architect: educational content development and branding
  • Private higher educational institutions: academic collaboration

Who it is for

  • Candidate or professional architectural draughtspersons, technologists, senior technologists and architects
  • Civil and structural engineers and technologists, quantity surveyors and land surveyors
  • Practising building designers seeking CPD credits
  • Firms integrating AI into practice, and academics and students exploring computational design and AI research

Programme details

Competence is demonstrated through challenges unique to each participant. All IBD courses are remote and undertaken online.

Part 1: Thinking

History, definitions and models of AI, Patrick Winston’s Triadic Model, and the mechanics of Large Language Models including tokenisation and temperature. First Principles Thinking applied to architectural reasoning. Sessions 1–2.

Part 2: Perception

Machine perception and learning: Linear Regression, K-Nearest Neighbour and Random Forests, then deep learning from perceptrons to LeNet and AlexNet, plus essential programming concepts. Sessions 3–4.

Part 3: Action

Supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning, training and testing datasets, tools versus technologies, and a reflection on meaning and value. Sessions 5–6.

Learning outcomes

  • Conceptual literacy: critically evaluate AI definitions, models and history in an architectural context
  • Analytical reasoning: apply First Principles Thinking to architectural problem identification
  • Technical application: design speculative or practical AI workflows integrating LLMs, diffusion models and other multi-modal systems

Evidence

Peer-reviewed assessment

Each participant identifies a significant challenge in their professional capacity. A board of members from across the built environment assesses the documented work against the application of knowledge, skills and experience. Upon a successful review, a certificate is provided confirming peer review.

CPD competency alignment

Specific to the South African Council for the Architectural Profession’s 10 Competencies, and aligned to Computer Applications and Architectural Design.

What participants receive

  • An IBD Peer-Reviewed Certificate of Competence endorsed by SAIBD, valid for Category 1 and Category 3 CPD points
  • Institutional recognition for participation in applied AI education within the built environment
  • An optional verified digital credential for professional portfolios and LinkedIn

Teaching methodology

Interactive live lectures, case studies and reading material, scenario-based problem solving with your own real-world challenges, and peer review and reflection.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is included in the programme?

Digital course access, mentorship and a certificate of completion are included in all fees. The programme runs as 8 live online sessions over 5 weeks, with extensive reading material provided.

Who is this product suitable for?

Architectural draughtspersons, technologists and architects, civil and structural engineers, quantity surveyors, land surveyors, practising building designers seeking CPD credits, firms integrating AI into practice, and academics and students.

How do I receive access after paying?

Seats are confirmed upon receipt of the registration fee. The full balance is due before the course commences. Contact admin@saibd.org.za if you need help.

Can I speak to someone before enrolling?

Yes. Email admin@saibd.org.za and our team will respond to your questions.

What is your refund policy?

The registration fee is non-refundable but may be transferred to a future intake if requested before the course start date. No refunds are issued after the second session. If the Institute postpones or cancels a course, participants are offered a full refund or placement in the next available session.

Are payment instalments available?

Yes. The 3-month payment plan is a R2,500 deposit plus 3 equal monthly instalments, with no interest.
