What Your Payment Will Enable You To Do

A reminder of what is included with your annual CPD Companion access:

  • All Cat 1 + 3 Points for the year: Fulfill your mandatory accredited learning activities and individual self-development requirements.
  • Guidance on how to claim Cat 2: Clear, step-by-step documentation on claiming workplace-based and professional practice points.
  • A VA membership (includes discounts): Voluntary Association membership recognized under Act 44 of 2000, unlocking exclusive industry partner discounts.
  • Self paced and live learning: Study at your convenience with digital modules and join live interactive sessions with leading built environment educators.
  • Certification and assisted tracking of points: Official verification logs recorded with SAIBD for straightforward annual SACAP profile renewal.
