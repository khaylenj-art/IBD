⚠️ Standard Payouts: 4–5 Business Days
  • Payout Speed: In South Africa, Paystack's default settlement timeline often takes 4 to 5 business days for standard accounts.

Strategic Gateway Recommendation

Always compare payout speed and total net transaction fees before switching gateways. For the Institute, Peach Payments provides the most balanced institutional settlement (Next-Day) with rich support for Capitec Pay and Ozow EFT, while Yoco is excellent for immediate same-day liquidity on card enrolments.

Institutional Frequently Asked Questions

Categorized guidance on academic requirements, CPD validation, payment plans, and course logistics.

1. Enrolment & Admission

The programmes are designed for candidate or professional architectural draughtspersons, technologists, senior technologists, architects, civil and structural engineers, quantity surveyors, land surveyors, and built environment firm leaders. Academics and postgraduate students are also welcome.
Intakes run monthly, quarterly, and bi-annually, calibrated to demand and faculty availability. Cohorts are strictly capped at 25 participants to preserve peer review quality.

2. Accreditation & SACAP CPD Credits

The South African Institute of Building Design (SAIBD) is a voluntary association officially recognised under the Architectural Profession Act No. 44 of 2000. It represents building designers and architectural technologists across South Africa.
Successful graduates earn 2 Category 1 CPD credits (Accredited Learning Activities) and 1 Category 3 CPD credit (Individual Activities), aligned directly with SACAP’s 10 Core Competencies for Architectural Design and Computer Applications.

3. Payments & Refund Policies

The R2,500 seat registration fee is non-refundable, but may be transferred to any future intake if requested in writing prior to the course start date. No refunds are issued after the second session has completed. If the Institute ever postpones or cancels an intake, enrolled participants receive an immediate 100% full refund or priority placement in the next session.
